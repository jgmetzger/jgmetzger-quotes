The Story

Lightning may never strike twice. But for the House of Fabrizio, success was a blessing that spanned four generations.

An ambassador, a fashion designer and a musical prodigy carry one family to fame and fortune beyond imagining. But jealousy and suspicion are never far behind success, and kidnapping, heartbreak and addiction threaten to bring the whole house of cards crumbling down.

Only by the grace of God could the family line be redeemed — and only by a humble heart could they discover the true riches that bring life, love and fulfilment.

The Author

Dieter Schaede

Grew up in war-torn Germany, left at eighteen for North America, made his name in fashion design and business before selling up to travel the world; settled on his favourite Caribbean island, St. Maarten, and founded a successful real estate company.

He and his wife Marisol raised five children and now divide their time between the Caribbean and the island of Mallorca, Spain; his life today is devoted to writing. Fabrizio is his third novel.
