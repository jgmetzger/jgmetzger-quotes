A few things to fix first.

I read the book and cover closely. None of this is big, and it's not a service I'm selling. It's just the right order to do things, because reviewers flag small errors.

On the cover

  1. 01Tagline reads a Novel based of true experiences. It should be "based on," or stronger, "inspired by true events" (the phrase the market knows). My suggestion: "inspired by true events".
  2. 02Author bio has children ,and, a stray space before the comma. Should read "children, and".

Make the brand agree with itself

  1. 03Pick ONE island spelling. The bio says St. Maarten; elsewhere it is Sint Maarten or St. Martin. My suggestion: standardise on "St. Maarten".
  2. 04Cover says Majorca, most copy uses Mallorca. My suggestion: use "Mallorca" everywhere.
  3. 05Positioning line differs: cover A Second Chance at Life vs site a fall from grace and path to redemption. My suggestion: lead with "A Second Chance at Life".

Inside the book (one proofread pass)

  1. 06Columbia should be Colombia; the name Cristina and Cristiana need to agree; carnegy hall → Carnegie Hall; to much time → too much; plus a few dropped words.
  2. 07All minor, all fixable in a single edit. Cheap to do, expensive to skip.
